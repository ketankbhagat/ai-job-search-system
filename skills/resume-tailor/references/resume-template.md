## Template Rules for AI

When tailoring this resume:

1. Preserve section names/order unless the user explicitly approves a change.
2. Never add a section just to hide missing experience.
3. Use only facts present in the private career profile/source resume.
4. Reorder truthful bullets and skills for relevance.
5. Do not invent metrics or outcomes.
6. Keep projects clearly labeled as projects.
7. Keep official titles/dates unchanged.
8. If a statement needs verification, flag it instead of silently inserting it.
9. Follow the employer's requested file format.
10. The final candidate, not AI, approves the resume.
