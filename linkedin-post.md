I built an AI job search system and I'm giving it away.

Not "spray 500 AI-generated applications." The opposite.

4 AI agents + Claude in Chrome + Google Drive. You stay in control.

Here's how it works:

You browse LinkedIn normally, logged into YOUR account, and hit Save on jobs worth a look. Then you open the Claude in Chrome extension and say "capture my saved jobs."

**Agent 1 — Capture**
Claude goes through your LinkedIn Saved jobs list and pulls every job into a Google Sheet tracker: company, size, title, location, posted date, applicant count. It also saves one Google Doc per job with the full description. No copy-pasting.

**Agent 2 — Profile**
Feed it your LinkedIn PDF, resume, and 3-5 real accomplishments. It builds your career evidence file, saves it to Google Drive, and suggests job titles you should actually target.

**Agent 3 — Fit**
Pick a row. Claude reads that job's doc and your profile from Drive and tells you: Prioritize / Apply / Stretch / Skip. Based on evidence, not a magic score.

**Agent 4 — Resume**
For the jobs worth pursuing — Claude tailors your resume using only real evidence from your profile. Saves it to Drive. Flags anything you need to verify.

**Agent 5 — You**
You review every line. You verify the job is real. You click Apply.

What this system will NOT do:
❌ Invent skills you don't have
❌ Scrape LinkedIn or automate activity
❌ Auto-submit applications
❌ Replace your judgment with a match score

What it WILL do:
✅ Turn your LinkedIn Saved jobs into a real tracker (Claude in Chrome)
✅ Save everything to your Google Drive automatically
✅ Turn scattered job hunting into a repeatable system
✅ Work with free tools only

The key insight: you already save jobs on LinkedIn. Claude in Chrome turns that Saved list into a structured tracker. It reads only the jobs you picked, in your own session, and never applies or messages anyone.

I built this because I watched fresh graduates either spray applications blindly or freeze because they thought they weren't "qualified enough."
