I built an AI job search system and I'm giving it away.

Not "spray 500 AI-generated applications." The opposite.

It's 4 AI agents that help you apply **better**, not more:

**Agent 1 — Capture**
Save jobs to a Google Sheet tracker. Structured. Searchable. No browser tab chaos.

**Agent 2 — Profile**
Feed it your LinkedIn PDF, resume, and 3-5 real accomplishments. It builds your career evidence file and suggests job titles you should actually target.

**Agent 3 — Fit**
Before you spend 2 hours tailoring, it tells you: Prioritize / Apply / Stretch / Skip. Based on your real evidence, not a magic score.

**Agent 4 — Resume**
Reorders your truthful experience for the role. Changes emphasis, not history. Flags anything you can't defend in an interview.

**Agent 5 — You**
You review every line. You verify the job is real. You click Apply.

What this system will NOT do:
❌ Invent skills you don't have
❌ Scrape LinkedIn
❌ Auto-submit applications
❌ Replace your judgment with a "match score"

What it WILL do:
✅ Turn scattered job hunting into a repeatable system
✅ Help you see which roles actually fit your evidence
✅ Save you from tailoring resumes for jobs you shouldn't apply to
✅ Work with free tools (Claude + Google Sheets + a browser)

I built this because I watched fresh graduates either spray applications blindly or freeze because they thought they weren't "qualified enough."

Both problems come from the same place: no system.

This fixes that.
