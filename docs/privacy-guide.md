# Privacy Guide

## What to Keep Private

**Never put these in the public GitHub repo:**

- Resumes and cover letters
- Your career profile (career-profile.private.md)
- Job tracker spreadsheet
- Recruiter messages and application confirmations
- Full job descriptions (may be copyrighted)
- Phone number, personal email, home address
- Date of birth, government IDs, financial information
- API keys, tokens, passwords

## What's Safe to Share Publicly

- The skill instructions (SKILL.md files) — they contain no personal data
- Blank templates — they have placeholder text only
- The README and guides — they describe the process, not your data

## AI Privacy Tips

Your AI assistant usually does **not** need:
- Your street address (city/region is enough)
- Date of birth
- Social Security number or government ID
- Banking information
- Passwords or API keys

Include contact details only when producing the final resume, not during analysis.

## Google Drive / Sheets Privacy

- Keep your tracker sheet set to **Private** (not shared)
- If using Claude's Google Drive connector, review what access you're granting
- You can always copy/paste instead of connecting Drive directly

## Job Scam Safety

Pause if a "recruiter" or "employer":
- Asks you to pay money to get hired
- Sends a check and asks you to buy equipment
- Requests bank details or ID unusually early
- Uses a look-alike email domain
- Pressures you to act immediately

FTC guidance: https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams
